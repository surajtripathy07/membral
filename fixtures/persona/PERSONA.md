# Elon Tusk — Persona Bible

> **Parody persona.** Elon Tusk is a fully fictional, affectionate comedic parody. He is
> not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a depiction of any real person or company. Every
> event, company, and character below is invented. See `README.md` for the honesty rule.

Elon Tusk is the demo persona for Membral: a synthetic AI-conversation corpus, run through
the **real** knowledge-graph pipeline, that becomes the face of the product (hosted demo,
launch video, README/gallery imagery, dev fixtures, eval golden sets). The founder's real
data never appears in public assets — Tusk replaces it, always labeled as a demo.

## 1. Biography

- **Name:** Elon Tusk (goes by "Tusk" internally; posts as `@tusk`).
- **Age:** 53.
- **Base:** Splits time between a rocket facility in the Texas scrubland and a car
  factory floor he refuses to leave. His stated permanent address is "the factory." His
  aspirational address is **Mars**.
- **Role:** Founder / CEO / self-appointed "Chief Meme Officer" of a sprawling empire (see
  companies below). He is technically the boss of everyone and the manager of no one.
- **Working style:** Runs six ventures simultaneously by context-switching every eight
  minutes and sleeping four hours a night on a factory beanbag. Manages by tweet. Believes
  every problem is a physics problem and every physics problem is a marketing problem.
  Genuinely brilliant on engineering fundamentals; catastrophically impulsive on
  everything social. Fires people in anger and rehires them by Friday.

### Voice / prompting style (how he writes to AI assistants)

This is load-bearing for realistic transcripts. Tusk has three registers:

- **Manic (default, ~50% of threads):** types fast, lowercase, drops units and vowels,
  no greeting, no thanks. *"gimme the delta-v for a fridge sized engine dont overthink it."*
  Peaks at 2–4am. Escalating exclamation as excitement rises.
- **Engineering-earnest (~30%):** when a real technical problem grips him he becomes
  detailed, precise, and asks genuinely sharp follow-ups. Signature move: **"explain X
  from first principles, assume I'm smarter than the textbook author."** Argues with the
  answer, then usually concedes the physics.
- **Meme-deflection (~20%):** when cornered, embarrassed, or losing an argument he retreats
  into meme-speak, emoji rationing ("one rocket emoji, no more"), and "add flamethrowers"
  as the punchline to any decision analysis.

Recurring tells across all registers: insists textbooks/regulations/CFOs are "being
cowards," ends serious plans with an unserious flourish, and asks for **"HR-safe" or
"legally-safe" rewrites of unhinged posts he has already sent.** When stressed (post-failure,
sleep-deprived) the mask drops and he is briefly, genuinely vulnerable — those are the
`emotional-processing` / Claude threads.

## 3. The 15-month timeline (2025-04 → 2026-06)

Arcs **evolve** and **intersect** — cross-area bleed is what gives the graph interesting
`contributes_to` / `discusses` edges. Key intersections are called out in *italics*.

### SpaceY — the rocket program (climax arc)
- **Apr–Jun 2025:** design the *Leviathan* Mars ship; stainless-hull and 42-engine
  decisions; first-principles combustion rants. *(→ Occupy Mars: it's a Mars ship.)*
- **Jun–Aug 2025:** engine tests, static fires, a turbopump eats itself.
- **Sep–Oct 2025:** first orbital attempt scrubbed on a valve → retry → **the glorious RUD**
  (rapid unscheduled disassembly) on **2025-10-02**. *THE setback.* *(→ Health: wrecks his
  sleep for weeks. → Zapp/Feud: Chad Vanguard gloats.)*
- **Nov 2025–Jan 2026:** post-mortem, first-principles rebuild, aggressive 90-day plan.
- **Feb–Mar 2026:** second attempt — partial success (lost after stage sep).
- **Apr–Jun 2026:** first full orbital success (**2026-04**); **booster catch with "the
  Claw"** (**2026-05**); reflight-cadence push. *(→ Occupy Mars: the timeline suddenly feels real.)*

### Tusla — production hell (parallel grind arc)
- Cyberwedge panel-gap and automation crisis; sleeping at the factory *(→ Health)*; a
  falling-panel **recall** (Jul–Aug 2025); a **robotaxi unveil that underdelivers** (Sep
  2025); the **"Optimist" humanoid demo caught being teleoperated** (Nov 2025); gigacasting
  cracks; a record quarter (Q2 2026). *(→ Finances: margins, Gary Puddle.)*

### Zapp — the social app spiral (chaos + feud arc)
- Post-acquisition advertiser exodus; rename to a glyph; firing/rehiring; **a reinstatement
  poll that backfires** (Aug 2025) *(→ Health: "my own poll made me the villain")*;
  everything-app / payments pivot; valuation crash (Nov 2025); the **Chad Vanguard feud**
  peaks around the RUD (Oct 2025) and again at dueling launches (Mar 2026); a cage-match
  challenge that never happens. *(→ MechaMind: Groak gets bolted into the feed.)*

### MechaMind / Groak — the AI embarrassment arc
- Found the lab (May 2025); GPU land-grab; defining "truthful" AI *(→ it keeps meaning
  "agrees with me")*; **Groak launches and immediately says something unhinged** (Sep 2025)
  *(→ Zapp, → Health: public embarrassment)*; alignment scramble; Groak 2 (Jan 2026, still
  weird); training-on-user-data ethics; agentic Groak takes over the Zapp feed (Apr 2026).

### The Dull Company — the tunnel bit
- Pitch tunnels to fix traffic; permit hell; "the test tunnel is just a car in a tunnel";
  the flamethrower-merch funding gag; and the grand **pivot to "interplanetary tunnels"**
  (Jan 2026) *(→ Occupy Mars: colonies go underground for radiation shielding)*.

### Occupy Mars — his "relocation" arc
- The persona's version of a city relocation is **personally moving to Mars**. Minimum-
  viable-colony design; radiation shielding; governance ("benevolent memelord");
  naming the first city; the wrenching **"should I personally move to Mars, leaving the
  kids?"** thread (Nov 2025) *(→ Fatherhood)*; Sabatier return-fuel chemistry *(→ SpaceY)*;
  first cargo-manifest planning after the orbital success (Apr–Jun 2026).

### Fatherhood — the personal-cost arc
- Comically many kids; spreadsheet co-parenting via Priya; naming a baby with a symbol
  (Sep 2025); **missing a recital for a static fire** (guilt); a child asking "why is the
  internet mad at you?" (Jan 2026); **a child's hospital scare** (Mar 2026, the most human
  thread) *(→ Health: he's running on no sleep during it)*; a letter to his kids about Mars.

### Health, Sleep & Biohacking — the body-as-machine arc
- Factory napping and back pain; a "need less sleep" biohacking stack; ice baths; a New-
  Year fitness kick with Rex Mountain; and two crisis spikes: **post-RUD insomnia** (Oct
  2025) and **crisis-addiction self-examination** (Feb 2026). Ends with a sincere long-
  horizon "don't destroy myself before Mars" plan.

**Cross-cutting theme:** *Finances* (CFO Gary Puddle, funding, margins, Zapp valuation, GPU
spend, the flamethrower unit-economics gag) runs through SpaceY/Tusla/Zapp/MechaMind as a
**secondary tag**, not a standalone cluster.
